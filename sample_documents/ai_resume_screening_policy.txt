AI RESUME SCREENING TOOL — SAMPLE POLICY

Purpose
This system helps recruiters prioritize applications. It provides recommendations and does not make final hiring decisions.

Privacy and personal data
The privacy notice explains that applicant personal data is collected for recruitment purposes. Applicants are informed about consent and data subject rights.

Security
Applicant records are protected using encryption, authentication, and role-based access control. Security controls are reviewed periodically.

Transparency
Recruiters are informed when AI is used. The system provides explainability information and discloses known limitations to users.

Human oversight
A recruiter performs human review before any employment decision. Cases can be sent for manual review and escalation.

Retention
The retention policy defines a retention period. Applicant data is deleted according to the data deletion procedure.

Fairness
The team evaluates fairness and bias across demographic groups and monitors for disparate impact.
